Privacy Policy — Nontakarn Portal

Nontakarn Healthcare Co., Ltd. & TIM Connection Co., Ltd. · Updated November 28, 2026

1. Application Overview

Nontakarn Portal is an internal operations and document management web application for authorized personnel of Nontakarn Healthcare Co., Ltd. and TIM Connection Co., Ltd.

2. Information Collected

When you sign in with Google, the portal requests only basic identity scopes (openid, email, profile) to receive your verified Google email address and display name.

3. How Information Is Used & Shared

Your Google email address is used strictly to verify access against our internal staff whitelist and enforce role-based permissions. We do not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties.

4. Data Retention & Security

Web session tokens expire automatically after 6 hours. You may sign out at any time or request access removal by contacting the administrator.

5. Contact

For privacy questions or access removal requests, contact closestbluewithme@gmail.com.
